# The Mirror Principle

- **LLMs are essentially mirrors**
- They mirror the skill of the operator
- Traditional experience ≠ AI competence
- "Someone can be highly experienced... but that does not mean they're skilled... now that AI is here"

<!-- 
This is the core insight - LLMs don't make everyone equally capable. They amplify existing skills and expose skill gaps. A highly experienced developer might struggle with AI if they don't understand how to effectively prompt and collaborate with these systems.

# The Skills Transition Challenge

- Traditional interviewing processes are "fundamentally broken"
- Skill assessment is now more complex
- Need new evaluation frameworks
- Continuous learning becomes critical

**Action Items:**
- Develop deliberate AI practice
- Focus on prompt engineering
- Understand AI capabilities and limitations
- Cultivate adaptability
