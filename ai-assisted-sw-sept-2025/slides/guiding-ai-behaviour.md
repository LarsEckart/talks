# Guiding AI Behaviour

AI tools rely heavily on your project's standards and conventions. Without clear guidelines, AI produces inconsistent or suboptimal code.

<br>

Think of your standards as **training data for your AI assistant**—the clearer and more consistent they are, the better the AI will perform.

<br>

<v-clicks>

- Coding style guides
- Architectural principles  
- Development processes
- Library usage guidelines

</v-clicks>

<!-- 

Speaker notes:

AI behavior is heavily influenced by the context it receives. Without explicit standards, AI makes assumptions that may not align with your project's needs. This goes beyond simple formatting rules.

When AI understands your architectural decisions, naming conventions, and preferred patterns, it becomes a force multiplier rather than a source of technical debt. But when standards are implicit or inconsistent, AI outputs become unpredictable, requiring more cleanup than they save in initial development time.

Standards should be comprehensive:
- Coding style guides (formatting, naming conventions, file organization)
- Architectural principles (how components interact, separation of concerns)
- Development processes (e.g., test-driven development, code review practices)
- Library and dependency usage guidelines (which libraries to use for what purposes)

The key insight: AI excels at following patterns. Give it clear patterns to follow, and it will consistently produce code that fits your project. Leave it guessing, and you'll spend more time correcting than creating.
