# Stop the bot when it starts to wander and get lost

<v-click>

**Treat it like your pair programming partner**

</v-click>

<v-click>

- Read (almost) everything it's doing, thinking, planning
- Press `ESC` to stop and inspect when you disagree
- Challenge approaches and provide better direction
- "You're absolutely right!"

</v-click>

<v-click>

**This is collaborative engineering, not delegation**

</v-click>
