# AI as Musical Instrument

<v-clicks>

- **Context matters**: Workplace vs. personal projects yield different experiences
- **Deliberate practice required**: Like learning guitar, AI needs intentional engagement
- **Experimentation is key**: "What they do is they play"
- **Patience with failure**: Initial struggles are part of the learning process

</v-clicks>

<!-- 
Huntley draws a compelling parallel between AI adoption and musical instrument mastery. # From Failure to Innovation

<v-clicks>

- **The TB303 Example**: Commercial failure → Creative revolution
- **Reframe challenges**: View obstacles as growth opportunities  
- **Persistent engagement**: Mastery through sustained, playful exploration
- **Discovery mindset**: "Have they discovered the beauty of AI?"

</v-clicks>
