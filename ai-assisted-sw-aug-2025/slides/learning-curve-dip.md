# The Learning Curve Reality

When adopting AI tools, expect a temporary performance dip

- Initial enthusiasm meets reality
- Old workflows disrupted before new ones established
- Time investment in learning pays off later
- The "J-curve" effect is normal and temporary

Key: Push through the dip to reach higher productivity
